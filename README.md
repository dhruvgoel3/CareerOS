# 💼 CareerOS

> **Open-source AI-powered career operating system for students, professionals, and lifelong learners.**

CareerOS is an open-source platform designed to help individuals plan, manage, and accelerate their careers using AI, data, and community.

Our mission is to make career growth structured, personalized, and accessible for everyone.

---

# 🤔 Why CareerOS?

Building a successful career is often confusing.

People struggle with:

- Choosing the right career path
- Learning relevant skills
- Building strong resumes
- Preparing for interviews
- Finding opportunities
- Tracking career growth
- Building professional networks

CareerOS brings everything together into one intelligent career platform.

---

# 🎯 Vision

Build the world's most comprehensive open-source career operating system.

A platform where anyone can:

- 🎯 Discover Career Paths

Empower every individual to take control of their professional journey.
